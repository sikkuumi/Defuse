Plans and pricing

The scanner is free. Teams pay for the tools around it.

Community is the whole scanner, open source, with nothing held back. The paid plans add tools for working through findings over time and across a team. They read what the scanner found; they never change what it found or how it is labelled.

The scanner decides

The paid tools read the scanner's own output. They organise findings - compare them, mark them, chart them - and never re-decide or relabel one.

Nothing is hidden

A finding your team marks is still listed, with the reason beside it. A comparison never calls a finding fixed; it says "no longer reported", because that is all it can know.

Your code stays with you

Everything runs on your machine or in your own CI. There is no account to make and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
